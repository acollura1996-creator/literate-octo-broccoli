Quests

Main Quest — The Tyrant of Kalenden

Kalenden rules this land from his walled citadel at its heart. Cross the moat at one of the four fords, break through his gates and slay Kalenden himself to claim his lands as your own.

  • Kalenden is guarded by his Death Guard, skeletal warriors and Dark Spires.
  • Every few minutes his Legion marches on one of the generals. Destroy Kalenden's Keep to stop the marches.

The Generals

Tips

  • Hero path: slay creeps for experience and gold, buy items from Goblin Merchants and Arcane Vaults, hire mercenaries, and use Fountains of Health. Your Altar revives your Hero.
  • Empire path: keep 5 Peasants on gold and the rest on lumber (build a Lumber Yard by the woods). Lay roads out from your Town Hall and build Houses touching them: a house only adds population while a road links it to your Town Hall. Advance through the Tribal, Feudal, Kingdom and Imperial Ages from the Town Hall to unlock new troops; houses, walls and gates rebuild themselves in each new age.
  • Edge outposts hold an extra gold mine, a mercenary camp, an arcane vault and a fountain, guarded by drakes and golems.

How to Play

Mouse

  • Left-click selects; drag to box-select; double-click selects all of a type.
  • Right-click moves, attacks, gathers, follows or picks up items. The label next to the cursor says what a right-click will do; with Peasants selected, trees light up under the cursor.
  • Roads and walls: choose them from the Peasant's build menu, then click and drag a line.
  • Shift queues orders or adds to the selection.
  • Mouse wheel or pinch zooms. Scroll with the screen edges, the arrow keys, middle-drag (grabs the map) or a two-finger trackpad swipe. Scroll speed is in the Menu (F10); fullscreen (⛶) makes edge scrolling easiest.
  • Click the minimap to move the camera; right-click it to send units.

Keyboard

  • Command card hotkeys are shown on each button (A attack, M move, S stop, H hold, Q W E R hero spells, O learn skill, B build).
  • F1 selects your Hero (twice to center). Ctrl+1–9 assign groups, 1–9 recall.
  • Tab cycles subgroup, Space jumps to the last alert, Backspace to your base.
  • Alt shows all health bars. Numpad 7 8 4 5 1 2 use inventory items.
  • F9 quests, F10 menu (pauses the game), F11 Generals board, Pause pauses.
